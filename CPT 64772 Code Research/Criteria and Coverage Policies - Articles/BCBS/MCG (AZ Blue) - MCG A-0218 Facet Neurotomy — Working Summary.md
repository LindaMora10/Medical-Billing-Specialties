# MCG A-0218 Facet Neurotomy — Working Summary

**Purpose:** internal billing reference for CPT 64772 (medial branch transection / rhizotomy), Dr. Timothy A. Luke, NPI 1437125846, Phoenix/Mesa AZ.
**Source:** MCG A-0218 (accessed via AZ Blue MCG portal, 09/22/2026). Proprietary MCG content — this is a paraphrased working summary, not a copy of the guideline. Pull the original from the portal for any appeal submission.

---

## 1. Approval criteria (all must be met)

**Pain**
- Chronic cervical or lumbar spinal pain, 3+ months' duration
- Cervical (e.g. post-whiplash) or lumbar only

**Failed conservative care** — at least one of:
- Exercise program
- Pharmacotherapy
- Physical therapy or spinal manipulation therapy

**Other causes excluded**
- Imaging and physical exam have ruled out disc herniation with concordant radicular pain, spinal stenosis with neurogenic claudication, fracture, and tumor

**Planned procedure** — one of:
- *Initial neurotomy:* both a first and a second diagnostic block, fluoroscopically guided, of the medial branches of the dorsal spinal nerves or intra-articular facet injections, each achieving at least 80% pain relief from baseline
- *Repeat neurotomy at the same level(s):* prior successful neurotomy with 50%+ pain reduction documented for at least 3 months

**Other**
- No coagulopathy
- No current infection

## 2. Scope limits that matter for 64772

| Item | What the guideline says | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| Method | Background describes neurotomy as thermocoagulation of the medial branches, performed percutaneously with an electrode under fluoroscopic guidance | Endoscopic / open mechanical transection is outside the described technique |
| Cervicogenic headache | Evidence graded insufficient, conflicting, or poor; net benefit not established | Do not use headache as the indication |
| Thoracic spine | Evidence graded insufficient, conflicting, or poor | Thoracic levels are not supported |
| Covered regions | Cervical and lumbar only | Limit claims to these |

## 4. Risk assessment for CPT 64772

**The gap:** MCG A-0218 is written around percutaneous radiofrequency thermocoagulation. Mechanical or endoscopic transection of the medial branch is not the technique described. A reviewer applying this guideline to 64772 finds no matching method.

**Likely outcomes:**
- Denial as not meeting A-0218 criteria (method mismatch), or
- Routing to experimental/investigational review

**Documentation that must be in the chart before the procedure:**
- [ ] Two diagnostic medial branch blocks, fluoroscopically guided
- [ ] 80%+ relief from baseline documented after each
- [ ] Blocks at least 2 weeks apart
- [ ] 3+ months of documented conservative care failure
- [ ] Imaging ruling out disc herniation, stenosis with claudication, fracture, tumor
- [ ] Cervical or lumbar levels only
- [ ] Every nerve transected named individually (unit support)
- [ ] Billable ICD-10 from the LCD's covered list (note: M54.5 is a non-billable category header)

**Recommendation:** obtain prior authorization or a written predetermination before scheduling. Do not bill 64772 to AZ Blue without one.
